# Mochi look mechanics

- The paws and lower torso stay anchored on one stable seated baseline.
- The large physical eyes lead the gaze; irises, pupils, eyelids, and highlights move together as intact eye surfaces.
- The nose and muzzle shift slightly toward horizontal directions, while the head turns only modestly.
- Ears and cheek fur follow the head with restrained secondary motion.
- For vertical directions, the chin lifts or lowers slightly and the eyelids expose the correct side of each eye.
- The collar stays attached and centered; the round tag may follow the chest by a few pixels but must not teleport.
- The tail remains connected and quiet so gaze direction, rather than whole-body rotation, stays readable.
- Avoid whole-sprite rotation, replacement pupils, face deformation, or broad raster warping.
